Know Your Rights

Every commuter deserves fair treatment and access to the transportation discounts provided by law.

Students

20% Fare Discount

Students are entitled to a 20% discount on regular public transportation fares during the entire period of enrollment, including weekends and holidays.

Legal Basis: Republic Act No. 11314 (Student Fare Discount Act)

Reminder: Present your valid school ID or current validated enrollment form, subject to applicable identification requirements.

Senior Citizens

20% Fare Discount

Senior citizens are entitled to a 20% discount on eligible public land transportation fares.

Legal Basis: Republic Act No. 9994 (Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010)

Reminder: Present an accepted senior citizen ID or other valid proof of eligibility.

Persons with Disabilities (PWDs)

20% Fare Discount

Qualified PWDs are entitled to a 20% discount on eligible public transportation fares.

Legal Basis: Republic Act No. 10754 and applicable transportation regulations.

Reminder: Present an accepted PWD identification document when claiming the discount.

Important: Discounts are subject to applicable laws, implementing rules, and transportation regulations. Double discounts are generally not allowed.
